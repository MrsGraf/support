Exam Training

1Comprehension

Show that you have understood the text and can present the relevant information clearly and concisely. Comprehension tasks test your selective understanding of the text: there will usually be information that should be left out. Pay close attention to the focus specified in the task and select only the information that directly answers it.

Task words

Operators

The operator tells you exactly what kind of response the task requires.

outlineGive the main features, structure or general principles of something.
summarize / sum upGive a concise account of the main points or ideas.
statePresent the main aspects briefly and clearly.
point outFind and explain certain aspects.
describeGive a detailed account of what somebody or something is like.

2Analysis

Examine how a text works. Support your findings with suitable textual evidence and explain how individual aspects contribute to meaning, effect or the overall argument. Always keep the specific focus of the task in mind rather than analysing every possible feature.

Task words

Operators

The operator tells you exactly what kind of analysis or explanation the task requires.

analyseDescribe and explain relevant aspects in detail.
examineDescribe and explain a particular aspect in detail.
explainMake something clear by giving reasons, details or relevant aspects.
compareShow relevant similarities and differences.
characterizeProvide a thorough analysis of a character.
interpretExplain the meaning, purpose or message of something.

From device to overall argument Move from identifying a stylistic device to explaining its effect and its contribution to the wider argument.

3Comment / Re-creation of text

Develop your own well-founded response or produce a text for a specific communicative situation. Pay attention to purpose, audience and text type and make sure that your ideas are relevant to the exact task.

Task words

Operators

The operator tells you whether you are expected to argue, evaluate or produce a particular text type.

comment (on)State your opinion clearly and support it with evidence or reasons.
discussGive reasons for and against an issue and come to a well-founded conclusion.
assess / evaluateExpress a well-founded opinion on the nature or quality of something.
write (+ text type)Produce a text with the specific features of the required text type.
Further support
Argumentative writing Plan and structure a comment, discussion or evaluation and develop convincing arguments. Writing a letter to the editor Purpose, structure, layout and useful language for a concise and persuasive letter to the editor.

The exact requirements always depend on the complete task. Use the operator together with the focus and wording of the assignment.
